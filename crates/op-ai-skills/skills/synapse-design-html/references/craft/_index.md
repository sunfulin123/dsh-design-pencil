## 1. Conflict Resolution Rules in This Domain

1. **Section Height Scope (`impeccable` Wins on `Operate`/`Read`):** `taste-layout-v4.md` and `taste-sections-plan.md` define a `100svh` (1-or-2 screen) section height grid designed specifically for **`Persuade` company landing pages**. Never force fixed viewport heights onto `Operate` dashboards, tools, or `Read` documentation.
2. **Hero-First Cold Gate (`hallmark` × `web-design-engineer`):** Build and verify the first viewport (Hero or primary workspace) before expanding the rest of a multi-section page.
3. **Context-aware craft:** Review elevation, hierarchy, numeric alignment and interaction against the selected DESIGN; do not treat fixed aesthetic bans as universal requirements.
