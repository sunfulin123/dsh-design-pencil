## 1. Conflict Resolution Rules in This Domain

1. **No Blocking `init` Interrogation:** `impeccable-new-work.md` and `impeccable-shape-discovery.md` are stripped of mandatory `PRODUCT.md` / `init.md` gates. Always start with Stage 0 autonomous workspace inspection and `ecc-direction-and-states.md`.
2. **Surface Mode Wins Over Category Habit:** `Operate` and `Read` modes (`surface-modes.md`) forbid decorative shaders, giant fluid display type, and page-load choreography—even for tech companies.
3. **Truth Over Invention:** `hallmark-brand-truth.md` and `taste-copy-rules.md` prohibit fabricated reviews, fake customer logos, or un-marked sample metrics (`data-assumed="true"`).
