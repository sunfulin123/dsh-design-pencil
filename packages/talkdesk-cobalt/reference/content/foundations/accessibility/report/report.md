## Talkdesk Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR)

ACRs are statements of conformance to accessibility standards that answer how the products meet the
criteria. ACRs can be created internally or by external accessibility experts and must always identify
that automated, manual and assistive technology testing was performed.

ACRs should have internal and external access because they inform about the degree of accessibility
conformance of products and also contribute to potential organizations'/customers'/users' decision-making
during the procurement process.

By default, the Talkdesk Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) uses the WCAG edition of the ITI
Voluntary Product Accessibility Template® (VPAT®), which is "the leading global reporting format for
assisting buyers and sellers in identifying information and communications technology (ICT) products
and services with accessibility features," and because — according to a training course created by the
Information Technology Industry Council (ITI) — WCAG level AA fully applies, without exceptions, all US
Revised Section 508 and EU EN 301 549 criteria for web software, web-based documentation and authoring
tool functionalities, converging with the current Talkdesk accessibility goals.

The international edition of VPAT® should be used if you need to:

* Evaluate non-web software, non-web documentation and/or closed functionality software (on products
  like ATMs, printers, etc.)
* Provide an ACR that fully evaluates all Section 508 (U.S.), EN 301 549 (EU), and W3C/WAI WCAG
  criteria

This is because WCAG doesn't fully apply to US Revised Section 508 and EU EN 301 549 criteria for
non-web software, non-web documentation and/or closed functionality software (on products like ATMs,
printers, etc.); however, the international edition is the only VPAT® edition that fully addresses all
US Revised Section 508 and EU EN 301 549 criteria, and its last version includes "the leading ICT
accessibility standards: Section 508 (U.S.), EN 301 549 (EU), and W3C/WAI WCAG."

Alternatively, or when requested, the 508 and/or EU editions could be used to provide separate reports.

Despite VPAT® being considered "the leading global reporting format" — and mentioned in the DHS OAST
Training Resources as "The ACR is created using a template called the Voluntary Product Accessibility
Template (or VPAT)" — we should keep evaluating new opportunities such as the new reporting methodology
suggested by Standard Accessibility Reporting (SAR).
