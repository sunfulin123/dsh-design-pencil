# Inclusive language

## Guidance

Talkdesk is a global company, so it's crucial that our communication remains free from bias. On this
living page you'll find some rules of conduct, as well as inclusive alternatives for terminology that
has been **widely** considered inappropriate or contentious.

* **Avoid making generalizations about people, countries, regions, and cultures.**
* **Avoid using slang,** especially if it could be considered cultural appropriation, such as "spirit
  animal".
* **Don't use profane or derogatory terms.**
* **Avoid culturally sensitive terms,** such as the terms associated with military or political actions
  or other historic events and eras.
* **Focus on people, not disabilities.** Don't use words that imply pity, such as "stricken with" or
  "suffering from". Don't mention a disability unless it's relevant.

| Do (inclusive language)                             | Don't (non-inclusive/contentious language)          |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary/replica Leader/follower                     | Master/slave                                        |
| Safelist/blocklist                                  | Whitelist/blacklist                                 |
| Team, everyone, people, y'all                       | Gendered nouns (e.g., guys)                         |
| Firefighter, chair                                  | Gendered nouns (e.g., fireman, chairman)            |
| They/them/their                                     | Gendered pronouns (e.g., he/him/his or she/her/her) |
| Synthetic, manufactured                             | Gendered adjectives (e.g., manmade)                 |
| People with disabilities                            | Handicapped, differently-abled                      |
| Woman/Women (when referring to someone 18 or older) | Girl/Girls                                          |
| Underrepresented groups                             | Minorities                                          |
