Pendo Guide is a 3rd-party platform used to help users through their first experience with our
products.

## Overview

User onboarding is a designed series of interactions and instructions that eases users into a
product's experience — from a simple greeting and explanation to a full series of guided tasks.

## Anatomy

**Welcome/Success message:** Illustration (clarifies or decorates), Title (welcome message subject),
Description (what to expect), Actions (a call to action related to the context).

**Tooltips:** Step numbers (total steps in the walkthrough), Step title (the step's objective), a
Description, and Actions.

### Variations

* **Action buttons** — the first step has only one action; every step after that has two (previous and
  next).
* **Tooltip positions** — top right, top left, side top left, and side top right, depending on where
  the highlighted component sits on screen.

## Usage

The Pendo walkthrough draws the user's attention to areas of interaction, buttons, or locations — most
useful on first access, before the user is aware of the platform's actions and features.

* The welcome message must always be centered.
* Tooltips must be positioned next to the highlighted component, feature, or action.
