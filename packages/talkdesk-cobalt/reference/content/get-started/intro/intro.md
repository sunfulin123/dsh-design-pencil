## Introduction

Let's start from the beginning and remind the vision and principles that guide our design system.

We are and we'll always strive to be trendsetters. We want to lead, not follow. Our design is brave,
modern, but not over-complex or unpredictable. Visual coherence, better UI decisions, better
communication, and efficiency are what define us and help us create incredible experiences for our
users. Driven by new challenges and ever-rising expectations, we'll keep evolving, supported by our
principles. These will never change.

### Get started

This documentation explains all the specs and proper use of the Cobalt design system components for
designers, developers, and interested parties.

The information here on the design system is not set in stone. It is a set of patterns, behaviors and
rules that any business unit should follow and use.

### Libraries

The Cobalt Design System is made of a few main libraries: design tokens, core components, patterns, assets
and helpers. [Design › Libraries](/get-started/design/#libraries) links each one in Figma, with the DesignOps
files behind them.

## Principles

We are and will always strive to be trendsetters. We want to lead, not follow. Our design is brave,
modern, but not overcomplex or unpredictable. Consistency, simplicity, and reliability define us and
help us create incredible experiences for our users. Driven by new challenges and ever-rising
expectations, we'll keep evolving, supported by our principles. These will never change.

* **Brave** — We dare to innovate. Our avant-garde vision gives users the chance to experience the
  future today.
* **Dynamic** — We embrace new challenges, pushing our limits to adapt to a rapidly changing
  environment.
* **Straightforward** — We favor simplicity to boost efficiency and make users' lives easier.
* **Reliable** — We seek to provide a consistent experience that delivers on its promises and builds
  trust.
* **Human** — We strive to understand our users' state of mind and connect with them empathically.
